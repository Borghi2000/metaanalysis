ABSTRACT: Prevalence of Burnout of Radiologists in Private Practice
Authors: Jay R. Parikh et al. (2023)
Journal: Journal of the American College of Radiology

Purpose: Evaluate prevalence/factors of burnout among private practice radiologists.
Methods: Survey of 1,235 radiologists (254 responses).
Results: Burnout rate 46%. Fulfillment rate 26.7%. Significant inverse association between fulfillment and burnout (r = -0.66). Nights/weekends (on call) associated with higher burnout.
Conclusions: Approx half of radiologists experience burnout. Self-care and older age are protective.
