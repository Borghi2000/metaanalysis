ABSTRACT: Bivariate analysis of sensitivity and specificity produces informative summary measures in diagnostic reviews
Authors: Reitsma JB, Glas AS, Rutjes AW, Scholten RJP, Bossuyt PM, Zwinderman AH (2005)
Journal: J Clin Epidemiol

Summary: Discusses the bivariate approach for DTA meta-analysis, which preserves the 2D nature of sensitivity/specificity pairs and incorporates correlation, improved over the traditional SROC approach.
