# Highlights

**Manuscript Title:** Diagnostic Accuracy of Multimodal and Generative Artificial Intelligence in Chest Radiograph Interpretation: A Systematic Review and Hierarchical Bivariate Meta-Analysis

**Target Journal:** *European Journal of Radiology Artificial Intelligence* (Elsevier, ISSN 3050-5771)

---

### Highlights (Strictly $\le 85$ characters per bullet including spaces)

- First bivariate meta-analysis of multimodal generative AI in chest radiography. (78 characters)
- Domain-adapted models showed higher sensitivity than general-purpose LLMs. (73 characters)
- Sensitivity collapsed in small lesions, advising against autonomous rule-out. (75 characters)
- Assisted second-reading reduces reporting time under mandatory human review. (77 characters)
- High between-study specificity variance was driven by extreme low prevalence. (76 characters)
